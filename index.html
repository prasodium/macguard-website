What it does

⚡

Charger detection

Notices the unplug right away, with an optional delay to avoid false alarms.

🔔

Loud alarm

Sounds on every unplug. Stops when you reconnect or confirm with Touch ID.

🔒

Screen lock

Locks your session and tells you if the lock was confirmed.

📍

Location

Adds a position to the alert, labelled fresh, cached or last known.

✉️

Email alert

Sent through your own Brevo account to a Gmail you verified.

🧭

Find My guide

One button opens Apple's Find My with the steps to recover your Mac.

Good to know

It cannot

  • Stop a shutdown or force-quit
  • Work while the Mac is off or asleep
  • Promise the alarm is audible

It does

  • Run fully on your Mac, no server
  • Keep your Brevo key in the Keychain
  • Email only an address you verified

Beta. Not yet tested on real hardware: many unplug cycles, a real email, the location prompt. Details

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, and open source. Brevo's free plan is enough for alerts.

Does it work without email?

Yes. The alarm and screen lock need no setup.

Why Touch ID or my password?

To stop the alarm, disarm, or change email settings. MacGuard never sees your password.
