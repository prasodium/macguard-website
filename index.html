Release notes

  1. Open the .dmg

    Drag MacGuard onto the Applications folder.

  2. Right-click, then Open

    Choose Open, then Open again. It is not notarized yet, so macOS asks the first time.

  3. Follow the setup

    Email is optional. The alarm and screen lock work without it.

Private by design.

Runs on your Mac

No server, no account, no tracking.

Key in the Keychain

Your Brevo key never leaves your Mac.

Verified address only

Alerts go only to an email you confirmed.

Questions.

Is it free?

Yes, and open source. Brevo’s free plan is enough for alerts.

Does it work without email?

Yes. The alarm and screen lock need no setup.

Why Touch ID or my password?

To stop the alarm, disarm, or change email settings. MacGuard never sees your password.
